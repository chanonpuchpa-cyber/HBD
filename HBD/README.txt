Birthday 3D Website

1) Extract the ZIP.
2) Put your photos in the images folder, named photo1.jpg through photo24.jpg.
3) Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome/Edge/Safari).
4) Tap the first screen to start.

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- images/ (add your photos here)

Notes:
- This is a CSS/JavaScript 3D-style photo globe and immersive photo wall, not a true volumetric 3D scan.
- Internet is needed for the Google Fonts import. The site still works without it using fallback fonts.
